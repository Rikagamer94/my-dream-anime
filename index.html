JOIN THE TEAM.

Welcome to the MY DREAM team application. This is a creative space for people who want to help build original anime stories, characters, animation, storyboards and the visual world around them.

Why this application exists

MY DREAM is designed as an original anime universe where stories, characters and visual ideas can grow into complete creative projects. A team makes that process stronger because different skills can work together: one person may be great at character design, another at animation, another at storyboarding, writing, editing, sound planning or visual development. This application helps us understand what you enjoy creating, what you can contribute, and what kind of projects you would like to work on. You do not need to present yourself as an expert. Clear communication, creativity, consistency and a willingness to learn are valuable parts of a creative team.

What can you gain from joining?

Being part of a creative anime project can give you practical experience working from an idea to a finished piece. You can practice turning concepts into visual material, learn how a storyboard connects to a scene, understand how character consistency matters across multiple shots, and develop better communication when working with other creators. Depending on the role and project, you may also have opportunities to contribute to original stories, character development, backgrounds, 2D animation, clean-up, inbetweens, editing, thumbnails, promotional artwork or other production tasks. The exact work is project-dependent, so the application is intentionally open to different skill levels.

Who can apply?

Anyone who genuinely wants to contribute to anime-style creative work can submit an application. Beginners are welcome, and previous professional experience is not required. If you are still learning, explain what you are currently practicing and show whatever work you are comfortable sharing. A small personal drawing, storyboard page, short animation test, character concept, background study or editing sample can communicate your creative direction. What matters is that the material represents your own work and that you are honest about your current level.

Creative roles

The team can involve many different areas. Possible roles include 2D animation, clean-up and inbetweens, storyboard, character design, background art, illustration, visual development, writing, editing, compositing, motion design, thumbnail creation and other creative support. You can apply for one role or describe a combination of skills. If your exact skill is not listed, write it in the form. The goal is to understand what you can make and where your creativity fits naturally within the production process.

What we expect from applicants

We value original work, respectful communication, reliable collaboration and a willingness to improve. If you are given a creative task, read the brief carefully and communicate when something is unclear. If you need more time, say so instead of disappearing. If you receive feedback, use it as part of the production process rather than treating every change as a rejection of your creativity. A professional workflow is not only about artistic skill; it is also about communication, file organization, consistency and completing agreed responsibilities.

Why your portfolio matters

Your portfolio does not have to be large. A few relevant examples are often more useful than a long collection of unrelated work. If you are applying for animation, show movement tests or short clips if available. For storyboard, show readable panels and clear shot progression. For character art, show characters from different angles or expressions when possible. For writing, provide a short original sample or describe a story you have developed. For editing or compositing, share an appropriate sample that demonstrates your technical approach. Only submit work that you have permission to share.

How the application process works

First, complete the form with accurate information about your creative interests and experience. Second, include a portfolio or sample link when you have one. Third, explain why you want to contribute and what you would like to learn. Applications can then be reviewed according to the needs of the current project. Submitting an application does not automatically mean that a person is selected for every project. Project requirements can change, and some roles may only be needed at certain stages of production.

Creative collaboration

Anime production is a connected process. A storyboard affects animation, animation affects editing, editing affects timing, and the visual style has to remain consistent from one scene to another. That is why communication between team members matters. The aim of MY DREAM is to build a workflow where creators can contribute their own ideas while still following a shared project direction. When everyone understands the goal of a scene, individual skills can work together instead of becoming separate pieces.

Originality and respect

Please submit only work that you created yourself or that you are authorized to use. Do not claim another creator's artwork, animation, writing or design as your own. If a sample contains third-party material, clearly explain what part you created. Respect for other artists is essential to building a sustainable creative community. The same principle applies to characters, stories, music, references and assets used in production.

What happens after you apply?

After you submit your information, the application can be used as a clear introduction to your creative profile. A review can focus on the role you selected, the examples you shared, your communication, and whether your interests match the needs of an upcoming project. You may be asked for additional samples or a small creative test when a project requires it. Any test should be based on the role and should have a clear purpose. If you are not selected for one project, that does not mean your creative work has no value; project requirements can change, and future opportunities may be different.

Build with consistency

Long-form anime projects are built one small step at a time. Character sheets, backgrounds, storyboards, key animation, clean-up, inbetweens, editing and final presentation all contribute to the finished result. The most useful team members are not necessarily the people who claim to know everything. They are creators who communicate clearly, keep learning, respect the shared direction and take care with the work they submit. If you are joining while still learning, be open about that. A transparent skill level makes it easier to assign suitable tasks and support your progress.

Show your creative identity

Use the application to tell us what makes your creative interests different. You might love cinematic backgrounds, expressive character acting, fast action cuts, emotional storytelling, clean linework, atmospheric lighting, storyboard composition or detailed world-building. There is no single correct way to be creative. The goal is to understand your strengths and interests so that future collaboration can be organized around real abilities rather than assumptions. Keep your answers genuine, concise where possible and focused on your own work.

Final note

MY DREAM is about turning imagination into something that can be seen, shared and developed over time. If you enjoy anime, storytelling, drawing, animation or creative production, this application is your opportunity to introduce your skills and your ideas. Whether you are experienced or still learning, explain what you can contribute today and what you want to learn next. A strong team is built through creativity, patience, communication and consistent effort.
